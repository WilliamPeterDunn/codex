# Initial project idea

> **Status:** Rough starting point, recorded from the user's idea. This is not yet a finalized specification.

## Concept

A site where African storytellers can upload stories in different media formats, including audio, video, images, and documents. Storytellers can attach attribution and licensing conditions to their work.

Users can search the stories and experience them in the original form or through a translation into another official language.

## Starting inspiration

This idea blends the earlier **StoryWeave** concept (stories and translations remain connected to the source) with **Consentful Story Kit** (creator attribution and reuse permissions).

## Open for refinement

The intended users, initial geography and languages, permission model, supported upload formats, translation workflow, and first prototype scope are all still to be decided.
